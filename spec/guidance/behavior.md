# Author a Behavior

A Behavior explains what a participant can accomplish or what the product makes
observable under stated conditions. Give an implementation worker enough product
meaning to make sound local choices without inventing the promise. “Add an
export endpoint” names work; the Behavior explains what someone obtains and
how they recognize completion or failure.

## Essential meaning

Make the intended outcome, applicable conditions and scope understandable.
Identify participants when their roles change the result or their authority to
request it. Explain the included behavior, the boundary of the promise, and
concrete observations that would contradict it. Preserve the product decisions
that matter while leaving implementation choices open where the promise allows.

Explain the underlying need when a source establishes it. Distinguish research
or stakeholder direction from a value hypothesis that remains untested. Reuse
an existing higher-level outcome that owns the enduring purpose; explain this
Behavior's contribution without duplicating that rationale.

## Keep the common shape

The [Knowledge contract](../spec/KNOWLEDGE.md#six-kinds) defines the sections and
essential meaning. Outcome carries the product result; Actors and Conditions
develop relevant participants and circumstances; Included and Excluded settle
scope; Examples illustrate distinctions; Falsifiers expose violations. Follow
the [body contract](../spec/KNOWLEDGE.md#body) when developing those sections.

Choose the information that resolves consequential ambiguity in this Behavior.
A section may need several paragraphs, scenarios, lists or supporting views.
Outcome, Included and Falsifiers are essential; other common sections may be
omitted when unnecessary. Prose needs no entry IDs unless a passage needs an anchor.
Optional family guidance can help the inquiry; use, adapt, combine or omit it
according to the subject. Its presence creates no additional required fields,
record types or validation rules.

Keep quality obligations and design decisions with their owning record kinds.
Explain their local relevance and declare appropriate relationships. Before
finishing, consider whether a plausible interpretation would disappoint the
owner because a consequential condition, result or boundary remains unstated.

The [shared authoring guidance](../GUIDANCE.md) explains judgment, sources and
record boundaries across all kinds.
